SDAIA Academy · Capstone

Catch the interaction before it reaches the patient.

A pharmacist-facing check that separates what the label says from what this patient can tolerate — and never quietly treats a missing value as a normal one.

  • Seven verified rules, every one sourced. Findings are ranked by severity and bound to product labelling, so you can check the claim rather than trust it.
  • Rules decide, the model only phrases. No language model determines whether a risk exists, how severe it is, or how it ranks. Its wording is validated before you see it.
  • Missing data is named, not assumed. Absent parameters are listed as “Not provided” and collected into a request list instead of being read as normal.
  • Upload a list, or type it. PDF, Word, CSV, JSON, plain text or a photo. Identifiers in the document are discarded at the door.

Educational and research prototype. Not an authorised medical device and not for clinical use. It supports, and does not replace, a licensed pharmacist and the treating prescriber. Enter de-identified data only.
