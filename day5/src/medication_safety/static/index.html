Medication Safety Review

Pharmacist-facing drug–drug contraindication and interaction check. The agent runs on the server; API keys and MCP tokens never reach this page.

Educational and research prototype. Not an authorised medical device and not for clinical use. It supports, and does not replace, a licensed pharmacist and the treating prescriber. Enter de-identified data only — no names, no record numbers, no dates of birth.
